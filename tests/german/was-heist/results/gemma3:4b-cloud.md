The grammar is **almost correct**, but it needs a slight adjustment.

The correct way to ask "What are you called?" in German is:

**Wie heißt du?**

Here's the breakdown:

* **Was** – means "what" (incorrect in this context)
* **Wie** – means "how" (correct – it’s the standard way to ask someone’s name)
* **heißt** – means "is called"
* **du** – means “you” (informal)

**Therefore, the corrected version is: Wie heißt du?**
