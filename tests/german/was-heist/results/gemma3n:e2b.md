The grammar of the German text "Was heißt du?" is **correct**.

It translates to "What is your name?" or "What do you call yourself?"

* **Was:**  This is a question word, meaning "what".
* **heißt:** This is the verb "heißen" (to be called) conjugated in the second person singular (du - you).
* **du:** This is the informal "you" pronoun.

Therefore, the sentence is grammatically sound and a common way to ask someone their name in German.
