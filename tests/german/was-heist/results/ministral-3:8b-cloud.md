The sentence **"Was heißt du?"** is grammatically correct in German, but it is **very informal** and could be interpreted in a few different ways depending on context:

1. **Literal meaning (question about pronunciation):**
   *"What does 'du' mean?"* (e.g., asking for the definition of the word *du*).

2. **Casual/playful greeting (like "Hey, what's up?"):**
   In some regions (especially Austria and parts of Germany), this can be used as a friendly, informal greeting—similar to *"What's going on?"* or *"How's it going?"*

3. **Potentially rude/aggressive (if tone is harsh):**
   In some contexts, it could sound like *"What do you think you're doing?"* or *"Who do you think you are?"* (depending on intonation).

### Suggested alternatives for clarity:
- If you want a **neutral question about meaning**:
  *"Was bedeutet 'du'?"*
- If you want a **friendly greeting**:
  *"Was geht?"* (very casual)
  *"Wie geht's?"* (more polite)
  *"Alles klar?"* (informal)
