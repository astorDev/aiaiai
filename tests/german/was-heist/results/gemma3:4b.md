The grammar in the text “Was heißt du?” is **incorrect**.

Here’s why and the correct way to say it:

* **“Was heißt du?”** literally translates to "What does *you* say?" This is a very strange and unnatural way to ask someone their name in German.

**The correct way to ask someone their name in German is:**

* **"Wie heißt du?"** (Pronounced: Vee height doo?) - This translates to "How are you called?" or "What is your name?"


**Explanation of the errors in the original:**

* **"Was"** (What) is used for asking about things or situations, not people's names.
* **"heißt"** (says, is called) is conjugated incorrectly for the informal “du” form.
