### Analysis of "Was heißt du?"

*   **Grammatical Error:** The main issue is the word order and the choice of the question word. While "Was" (What) is possible in a very literal sense ("What are you called?"), it sounds unnatural and slightly awkward in German.
*   **Correct Question Word:** The standard and natural question word for asking someone's name in this context is **"Wie"** (How).

### The Best and Most Common Correction

The correct and most common way to ask "What is your name?" informally (using "du") is:

**Wie heißt du?**

*(Literally: "How are you called?")*
