The sentence **"Was heißt du?"** is grammatically correct in German, but it is **very informal and potentially rude** in most contexts.

### **Grammatical Analysis:**
- **"Was"** = Interrogative pronoun ("what")
- **"heißt"** = Present tense of **"heißen"** ("to be called")
- **"du"** = Informal "you" (singular)

The sentence literally means:
**"What do you call yourself?"** (or **"What's your name?"** in a very casual way).

### **Better Alternatives (More Polite & Natural):**
1. **Formal (polite):**
   - **"Wie heißt du?"** (informal, but standard)
   - **"Wie ist Ihr Name?"** (formal, for strangers/elders)

2. **More natural in conversation:**
   - **"Wie heißt du?"** (most common)
   - **"Wie ist dein Name?"** (slightly more natural than *"Was heißt du?"*)

### **Why "Was heißt du?" sounds odd:**
- **"Was"** is usually used for **objects** (e.g., *"Was ist das?"* = "What is that?").
- **"Wie"** is the standard way to ask about **names, ways, or manners** (e.g., *"Wie geht’s?"* = "How are you?").

### **Conclusion:**
- **Grammatically correct?** ✅ Yes, but **uncommon and potentially impolite**.
- **Better to use:** **"Wie heißt du?"** (unless you're joking or being sarcastic).
