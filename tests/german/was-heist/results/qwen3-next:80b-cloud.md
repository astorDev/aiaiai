The phrase **"Was heißt du?"** is grammatically incorrect. The correct way to ask "What is your name?" in German is:

**"Wie heißt du?"**  

### Explanation:
- **"Wie"** (meaning "how") is used in this fixed expression to ask for someone's name.  
- **"Was"** (meaning "what") is incorrect here because the phrase literally translates to "How are you called?" (a common German construction for asking names), not "What are you called?"  

### Correct Examples:
- ✅ **"Wie heißt du?"** (informal, singular)  
- ✅ **"Wie heißen Sie?"** (formal/polite)  
- ✅ **"Was ist dein Name?"** (less common, but grammatically correct; literally "What is your name?")  

However, **"Wie heißt du?"** is the most natural and widely used form in everyday German.
