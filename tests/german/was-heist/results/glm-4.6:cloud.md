### The Grammatical Part

Grammatically, the sentence is perfectly fine.
*   `du` is the 2nd person singular pronoun ("you", informal).

### The Usage Part (The Important Part)

In German, you use the question word **`Wie`** (how) to ask for a name, not **`Was`** (what).

The correct and natural way to ask for someone's name is:

> **Wie heißt du?**

This literally translates to "How are you called?", but its idiomatic meaning is "What's your name?".

### Why "Was heißt du?" is wrong in this context

Using "Was heißt du?" would be understood, but it sounds very odd. It's like asking:
*   "What is the literal meaning of the word 'you'?"
*   "By what title are you known?"
*   "What do they call you?" (which in English often implies a nickname or a reputation, not a given name).

It's simply a fixed phrase in German that you use **`Wie`** to ask for a name.

### When to Use "Was heißt...?"

You use **"Was heißt..."** when you are asking for the **meaning or translation of a word**.

> **Was heißt** "Apfel" **auf Englisch?**
> (What does "Apfel" mean in English? / What is "Apfel" called in English?)

The answer would be:
> **"Apfel" heißt** "apple".
> ("Apfel" is called "apple".)
