The grammar in "Was heißt du?" is **incorrect**.

**Correct version:**
```
Wie heißt du?
```

**Explanation:**
- "Was" means "what" and is used for asking about things/objects
- "Wie" means "how" and is used for asking about names
- The correct phrase "Wie heißt du?" literally translates to "How are you called?" which is the proper way to ask someone's name in German

This is a common mistake for English speakers, as "What's your name?" seems like it should translate to "Was heißt du?" but German uses "wie" (how) instead.
