**Why the original is not grammatical**

| Original | Problem | Explanation |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| **Was** | Wrong interrogative word | *was* is used for things or concepts (“what?” in the sense of “which thing?”). When asking about a person’s name, German uses **wie**. |
| **heißt** | Correct verb form, but placed with the wrong question word | *heißen* (to be called) is fine, but it must be paired with the appropriate interrogative. |
| **du** | Correct pronoun for “you (singular, informal)” | No issue here; the pronoun is appropriate for addressing someone informally. |

**Other possible ways to ask the same thing**

- **Wie heisst du?** (without the umlaut – acceptable, but the standard spelling is **Wie heißt du?** with **ß** only in certain regions; in most modern German writing the umlaut **ä** is retained.)
- **Wie ist dein Name?** – “What is your name?”
- **Wie nennt man dich?** – “What is your name?” (more colloquial)

So, replace **Was** with **Wie** to make the question grammatically correct.
