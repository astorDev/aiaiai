The correct way to ask "What is your name?" in German is:

**"Wie heißt du?"**

### Explanation:
- **"Was"** means "what" in the sense of objects or things (e.g., *Was ist das?* – "What is that?").
- **"Wie"** (how) is used when asking for names (*Wie heißt du?* – "What are you called?" / "How are you named?").
- **"Heißt"** is the correct conjugation of *heißen* (to be called) for the informal "you" (*du*).

### Alternative formal version:
If you're addressing someone formally (e.g., an adult you don’t know), you’d say:
**"Wie heißen Sie?"** (using *Sie* instead of *du*).
