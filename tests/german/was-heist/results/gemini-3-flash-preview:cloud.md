The sentence is **grammatically incorrect** if you are trying to ask someone for their name.

### The Correction
The correct way to say "What is your name?" in German is:
> **Wie heißt du?**

### Why?
In German, you use the question word **"wie"** (how) instead of "was" (what) when asking for a name. 
*   **Wie** (How) + **heißt** (are called) + **du** (you)?
*   Literal translation: *How are you called?*

### Other alternatives:
*   **Wie ist dein Name?** (What is your name? - informal)
*   **Wie heißen Sie?** (What is your name? - formal)
*   **Wer bist du?** (Who are you?)

**Note on "Was heißt...":**
You only use "Was heißt..." when asking for the meaning of a word. For example:
*   *Was heißt „Auto“ auf Englisch?* (What does "Auto" mean in English?)
