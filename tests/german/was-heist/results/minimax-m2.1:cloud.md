# German Grammar Check

## Input
> Was heißt du?

## Analysis

This sentence contains a grammatical error. The verb **heißen** (to be called) requires a subject that can logically be described as being "called" something.

## Issues

1. **Incorrect word order/usage**: The sentence asks "What means you?" literally, which doesn't make sense in German.

2. **Wrong construction**: The verb "heißen" works like this:
   - "Ich heiße Maria" = My name is Maria (I am called Maria)
   - "Wie heißt du?" = What are you called? (What's your name?)

3. **Meaning mismatch**: The sentence doesn't correctly express "What do you mean?"

## Corrections

Depending on your intended meaning:

- **"What does this mean?"** → **Was heißt das?** or **Was bedeutet das?**
- **"What do you mean?"** → **Was meinst du?**
- **"What's your name?"** → **Wie heißt du?** or **Wie heißt du?**

## Corrected
**Was bedeutet das?** or **Was meinst du?**
