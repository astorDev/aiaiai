## TL;DR

The article discusses 3 essential topics about agentic-driven development, covered by Uncle Bob in his latest interview. The take-home messages would probably be the following:

1. AI agents are as subject as humans are to messy code, therefore fundamentals still apply.
2. A detailed, waterfall-style planning approach fails for agents, just like it does for humans.
3. The way a programmer should learn is still by writing code by hand.

Robert Martin lived through many evolutions of the art of software engineering. For me, it brought a lot of optimism to hear his reflective thoughts on the current AI era in comparison to all the other transformations:

> We are now at the abstraction level above the compiler. It used to be the compiler. Before that it was assembly. Before it was binary... Every time people at the lower level complain: "Oh, this is going to ruin everything!"
